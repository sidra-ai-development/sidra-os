# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

The SIDRA GitHub community is for constructive technical discussion, product feedback, workflow sharing, and support between people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Please be respectful, specific, and helpful. Critique ideas and behavior, not people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning language, deliberate disruption, spam, publishing another person's private information, or posting credentials, secrets, payment information, private files, or security vulnerabilities.

## Scope

This applies to Issues, Discussions, pull requests, and other community spaces maintained by SIDRA AI Development.

## Reporting

For community conduct concerns, contact **support@sidra-ai.com**.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting details publicly.
